# OSRS Market Tracker

> **In Development** - This project is actively being developed and features may change.

A market tracking application for the **Old School RuneScape Grand Exchange**, designed to make it easier to understand item prices, track investments, and identify market trends.

## Goals

The OSRS Grand Exchange contains a huge amount of market data, but it can be difficult to turn raw prices into useful information.

OSRS Market Tracker aims to provide one place to:

- Track current and historical Grand Exchange prices.
- Monitor favourite items through watchlists.
- Track virtual holdings, value changes, and profit/loss.
- Identify useful market trends and price movements.
- Provide market insights without requiring users to interpret raw price data themselves.

## Planned Features

-  Historical item price tracking
-  Item watchlists
-  Virtual portfolio and profit/loss tracking
-  Price trends, highs/lows and market statistics
-  Price and market alerts
-  AI-assisted market insights

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Kotlin, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Docker
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript 
- **AI:** Python, FastAPI, LLM-powered market insights *(planned)*
